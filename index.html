about

the kind of place where we’ll remember your name (and your order).

first came the ideas. then the sketches. then, slowly, it started coming to life. le bun is a neighbourhood bakery in mount austin, johor bahru, made for the everyday ritual of bread and coffee shared with familiar faces.

01 / 06
  1. a facade in warm wood, a nod to the french boulangeries that got us into this in the first place.
  2. green, because that’s what a neighbourhood feels like to us. fresh, familiar, grown slowly.
  3. every corner has a story behind it. the counter, the shelves, the little details you’ll walk past without noticing.
  4. fresh bread, made for the everyday.
  5. good coffee, worth slowing down for.
  6. familiar faces, starting with yours.
